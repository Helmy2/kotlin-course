## Curriculum Architecture

This course is structured across **3 Core Days** spanning 9 hands-on concepts, moving from foundational syntax and expression-oriented design to compile-time null safety, polyglot JVM interoperability, and high-throughput structured concurrency.

## Interactive Learning Tools

Explore the course reference tools designed to accelerate your mastery of backend Kotlin:

- **[Branch Catalog (18 Branches)](/kotlin-course/reference/branches/):** Searchable engineering ledger of all 9 starter and solution branch pairs with 1-click `git switch` checkout commands.
- **[Kotlin Backend Master Index](/kotlin-course/reference/master-index/):** Searchable matrix covering Null Safety operators, Scope Functions (`let`, `apply`, `run`, `also`, `with`), and `@Jvm*` annotations.
- **[Coroutines & Concurrency Guide](/kotlin-course/reference/coroutine-wizard/):** Interactive breakdown of Coroutine builders, scopes, dispatchers, cancellation rules, and resilient fallback patterns.
